This is the bxHive blog — a place for notes on building an audit-trail-first
replacement for the behavioral economics lab.

## What you'll find here

We'll write about the things we're working through as we build the platform:

- **Protocol design** — how an experiment goes from a hypothesis to a
  reproducible, on-chain manifest.
- **Cryptoeconomics** — escrow-first funding, automatic settlement, and why the
  experimenter never holds participant funds.
- **Replications** — running classic studies (the Trust Game, BRET, Dictator)
  and what we learn doing it in the open.

## Why a blog

Open science is the whole point of bxHive, and that extends to how we build it.
Expect short, concrete posts — design decisions, results, and the occasional
dead end.
